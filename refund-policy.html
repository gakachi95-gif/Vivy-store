Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026 — placeholder date. Update this whenever the policy changes.

Our approach

Because Vivy products are digital and delivered instantly, refund requests are handled case by case. This is a template policy — set the specific refund window and conditions that fit your business before launch.

Eligible situations

  • You were charged but never received access to your product due to a technical error
  • The product delivered was significantly different from its description
  • A duplicate purchase was made by mistake

Not typically eligible

  • Change of mind after downloading and accessing the full product
  • Requests made long after the purchase window defined by Vivy

How to request a refund

Contact Vivy through the Contact page with your order details and the reason for your request. Vivy will review and respond within a reasonable time.

Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through the connected payment provider once that integration is active.
