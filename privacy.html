Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 — placeholder date. Update this whenever the policy changes.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Vivy collects when you use this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. This is a template policy — replace it with a policy reviewed for your specific business and region before accepting real payments.

Information we collect

When you browse Vivy, we may collect basic usage data (such as pages visited) through standard web analytics if enabled. When you make a purchase or contact us, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address and country. Vivy does not collect or store payment card details directly — that is handled by a connected payment provider.

How we use information

  • To deliver the digital products you purchase
  • To respond to support and contact requests
  • To send newsletter updates, only if you opt in
  • To improve the Vivy website and product catalog

Cookies and local storage

Vivy uses your browser's local storage to remember items in your shopping cart. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to Vivy's servers by this website alone.

Third parties

If Vivy connects a payment provider (such as Flutterwave), email service, or analytics tool, those providers will process certain data under their own privacy policies. Vivy will link to those policies here once connected.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from Vivy emails at any time. You can contact Vivy to ask what information is held about you or to request its deletion, subject to applicable law.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Contact page.
