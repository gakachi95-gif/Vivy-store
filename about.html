About Vivy

Digital opportunities, made easier to act on.

Vivy exists to make digital opportunities easier to understand and easier to act on.

Our mission

Vivy creates practical resources for people who want to learn digital skills, create online, use AI effectively, grow their presence and build digital income streams. We believe the biggest barrier isn't information — it's turning information into action.

That's why every Vivy product is built around one idea: Learn → Apply → Build → Grow. Not theory for its own sake, but resources you can actually use this week.

What we make

Vivy designs guides, templates, prompt packs, planners and toolkits across the areas that matter most to today's online creators and entrepreneurs: making money online, using AI and automation, growing on social media, digital marketing, content creation, templates, business systems and productivity.

How we work

Vivy is a single, focused brand — not a marketplace of many sellers. Every product on this site is created and maintained by Vivy, so you always know what you're getting: practical, tested, and built with the same standard from one product to the next.
